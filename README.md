### [Quil](./quil)
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-beta-orange?style=flat-square) ![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-macOS-blue?style=flat-square) ![LLM](https://img.shields.io/badge/LLM-Qwen3_4B-purple?style=flat-square) ![Privacy](https://img.shields.io/badge/privacy-100%25_local-brightgreen?style=flat-square)

> **A private, local AI companion that learns you over time.**
> Everything runs on your device. Nothing leaves your machine. Ever.

Quil is a **desktop AI assistant** built with Tauri and Python that runs entirely on your computer. There is no cloud reliance, no API keys, and no subscriptions. It is a system with memory, autonomy, and self-improvement built into its core.

**Core Features:**

* **7-layer memory system:** Inspired by human cognition. It includes episodic, semantic, procedural, prospective, emotional, contextual, and working memory.
* **Workshop:** Delegate complex tasks (e.g., "build me a resume"). Quil plans and executes them in the background while you keep chatting.
* **Self-improving prompts:** Every hour, Quil evaluates its own responses and rewrites its instructions based on what works.
* **Interactive memory graph:** A force-directed, canvas-rendered visualization of everything Quil knows about you.
* **100% private:** Your conversations, documents, and data never leave your local machine.

**Tech Stack:** Tauri 2.0 (Rust) | Python FastAPI | Qwen3 4B via Ollama | LanceDB | SQLite | D3.js
